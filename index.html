Privacy Policy — Shotodo

Effective date: July 13, 2026

Shotodo (“the app”) is developed and published by Derva (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes. The short version: we don’t collect your data — we don’t even have servers.

What we collect

Nothing. Shotodo has no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, and no backend. We never see your screenshots, your extracted text, your reminders, or anything else.

Screenshots and text recognition

When you select a screenshot for analysis, text recognition (OCR) runs entirely on your device using Apple’s Vision framework. Your images never leave your iPhone.

Text sent to AI providers

To classify what a screenshot means, the app sends the extracted text only — never the image — to the AI provider you select:

  • Groq (default, using the app’s built-in free tier or your own key)
  • OpenAI, Anthropic, Google AI, or xAI (only if you add your own API key)

This text is transmitted directly from your device to the provider you chose, and its handling is governed by that provider’s privacy policy: Groq, OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, xAI. We are not a party to that transmission and receive no copy of it. Avoid analyzing screenshots containing information you would not send to your chosen provider.

API keys

API keys you add are stored in the iOS Keychain on your device. They are sent only to the provider they belong to, to authenticate your requests. They are never transmitted to us or anyone else.

Reminders, Calendar, and Contacts

The app writes to Apple Reminders, Calendar, and Contacts only with your permission and only when you tap Save on the confirmation screen. That data lives in Apple’s apps, under Apple’s privacy protections. Shotodo keeps no copy beyond your on-device history.

Analysis history

Your last 50 analyses (compressed thumbnail, extracted text, and results) are stored locally on your device so you can review them in the Activity tab. You can delete individual entries or clear the whole history at any time. This data never leaves your device.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through your Apple Account. We never see your payment details. Manage or cancel anytime in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions.

Children

Shotodo is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly process data from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. Material changes will be noted in the app’s release notes.
